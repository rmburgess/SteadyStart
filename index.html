Find a provider

These are pregnancy care clinics in Texas. Each one says on its own website that its care is trauma-informed. That means they try to make care feel safe for people who have been hurt. We have not tested or rated them.

Calling a clinic yourself

Before you make a visit, you can ask:

  • "Are your doctors trained in trauma-informed care?"
  • "Can I bring a person I trust to every visit?"
  • "Can I ask for a woman or a man to care for me?"
  • "Can I keep my clothes on and talk first?"
  • "Can you write down what I need, so I don't have to say it again?"

If they answer kindly and don't rush you, that is a good sign.

Why this guide exists

If you need someone now

  • National Domestic Violence Hotline, call 1-800-799-7233 or text START to 88788
  • RAINN National Sexual Assault Hotline, call 1-800-656-4673
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, call or text 988
  • In an emergency, call 911.
